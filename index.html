Frequently Asked Questions

Is PassSnap really free?▾
Yes — completely free with no hidden charges, subscriptions, or watermarks. The core tool will always be free.
Is my photo uploaded to a server?▾
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photo never leaves your device and is never sent to any server — making PassSnap fully private and GDPR-compliant.
What size should I print the file?▾
Print at actual / 100% size on a 4×6 inch (10×15 cm) photo paper at 300 DPI. Do not scale the image — the photos are precisely sized to match official requirements.
What background color should I use?▾
Most countries (US, UK, EU, India, Australia) require a plain white or off-white background. Always verify with your country's official immigration website before printing.
My country isn't listed — can I use a custom size?▾
We're actively adding more presets. In the meantime, find your country's official dimensions and choose the closest matching preset. We're working on a custom size input feature.
